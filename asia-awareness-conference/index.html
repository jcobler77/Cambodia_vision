Why Asia

Where the need is greatest, and where the family is already sending

Asia is home to the largest unreached and unengaged populations on earth. Billions of people live, work and raise their families there without a church nearby, a Bible in their language or a neighbor who follows Jesus.

India carries the greatest need of all, with more unreached people groups than any other nation, at a moment when global attention to it is fading. Cambodia, home to the Khmer, one of the 100 largest unreached people groups in the world, is where Living Water in Olympia already has a long-standing partnership and friends we know by name.

We aren't starting from scratch, and we aren't going alone. Churches in the Philippines and across the global Foursquare family are already sending missionaries throughout Asia. This conference is a chance to learn from them, pray with them, and find our part in a mission that belongs to the whole church.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Who should come?

Pastors, regional pastors, church leaders, church members and anyone preparing for or already serving in missions. If you care about the people of Asia, or want to find out whether you should, you're welcome here.

How much does it cost?

Full Conference pricing is still being finalized and includes both dinners. Evening Sessions Only is $20 with a meal when you register in advance, or $5 at the door for the session only. If cost is a barrier, please reach out.

Are meals included?

Full Conference registration includes Monday's welcome dinner and Tuesday's Indian dinner. Tuesday lunch is on your own during a two-hour window, with plenty of options nearby. Evening registration ($20) includes dinner.

Where should I stay?

See our list of nearby hotels. Host homes with Living Water families are also available on request. Just let us know when you register.

What should I bring?

A Bible, a notebook or device for notes, a water bottle and layers for Northwest spring weather. Bring a friend or a team from your church, too.

How can I get my church involved?

Bring a team, pray with us, or talk with FMI Asia leaders at the conference about partnering with a field, supporting a missionary or sending a short-term team. Email us and we'll help you take the next step.
