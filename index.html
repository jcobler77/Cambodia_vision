The Why

A young nation at a hinge moment

Cambodia carries a hard history — and a hopeful present. After the Khmer Rouge, the church numbered barely 250. Today it's 500,000 — and a movement of leaders is believing for 2 million, one-tenth of the nation, to follow Jesus by 2033, in a country where most people are under thirty. Killing fields to living fields. We'll see it up close: schools and skills training, kids' classes near the border, families being cared for, churches and YWAM bases doing the slow, faithful work of the Kingdom.

The Week

Day by day

Full days, some long drives, real ministry. Here's the shape of it (tentative, and being finalized with our in-country hosts).

Sun Arrive Phnom Penh

Land in Phnom Penh, transfer to the hotel, and settle in. Welcome dinner in the evening — meet the team you'll travel with and hear the shape of the week.

Mon Cambodia's story & the 2033 vision

Morning at the Killing Fields and S-21 — the ground that makes the hope make sense. Evening at the New Life Educational Center: skills training, youth engagement, and a first look at the vision taking root.

Tue Battambang & Poipet

Drive to Battambang (~3.5 hrs); lunch at Café Eden and a campus tour. Continue to Poipet for the afternoon — kids' classes and the community education hub near the Thai border.

Wed The border camp → Siem Reap

Morning food and school-supply distribution at the displaced-families camp. Then drive to Siem Reap (~2 hrs) to regroup.

Thu Siem Reap: wonder, work & worship

Angkor Wat in the morning. Afternoon of staff encouragement at the YWAM Siem Reap campus. Evening community night (~70 people) — worship, stories, and a send-off.

Fri Depart

Transfer to Siem Reap International (REP) and head home carrying what you've seen.
